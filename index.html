⚙ System Settings

Changes are saved automatically and apply to everyone. Theme, appearance and the settings lock are per device.

🔐 Admin Security

Protect destructive actions (reset, restore, permanent delete) and lock the settings page with an admin PIN (4–6 digits). The lock applies to this device only and re-engages automatically after 5 minutes.

Opens Finance on the Orders page · must differ from the admin PIN

Add sign-in accounts, choose who may use FleetGuard and the Orders page, turn accounts off and send password-reset emails — without opening Firebase. Locks again after 5 minutes or when you leave Settings.

🎨 Display & Theme

Colour theme and appearance are saved on this device only — they never change what other users see.

🏢 Company Profile

Used on reports, exports, the sidebar and the login screen. Leave a field empty to keep the built-in default.

PNG or JPG, compressed to ≤ 200 KB. Appears on every printed report.

🎯 Risk Thresholds & Retention

Risk score = high 10 · medium 5 · low 2 per violation. Drivers at or above the medium score are Medium risk; at or above the high score (or with the given number of high-severity violations) are High risk.

Driver Statuses

Violation Types

Define violation categories and default severity.

Job Card Management

Manage maintenance services, default intervals and apply services to trucks. Applied services update truck history and any active job card services.

Truck Document Types

Custom Fields

📁 Import attachments

Add PDFs or photos — single files or a whole folder — to drivers, trucks and trailers. Open to everyone: no admin PIN needed, even while Settings is locked.

The JSON backup contains every collection (drivers, trucks, trailers, orders, job cards, HSE, incidents, audit trail, recycle bin, settings) and can be restored below. The Excel export is for reading in a spreadsheet only.
